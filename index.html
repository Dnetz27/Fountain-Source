Water, engineered.

Fountain Source is a consulting firm, with over 30 years of experience, that specializes in designing and engineering fountains, water features, swimming pools and spas for public, private and commercial environments.

Mission

"Our success is measured in having our clientele re-consider us for future endeavor."

In keeping true with this statement, we continually strive to develop and evolve the services we provide our clients. To accomplish this, we have established, and refined, a culture based on three core values: integrity, quality and respect.

Consulting services

  • Mechanical engineering
  • Electrical and controls engineering
  • Concept design and feasibility
  • Structural design
  • Building Information Modeling (BIM)
  • Peer review
  • Order of magnitude cost estimating
  • Project management
  • Bid analysis and negotiation
  • Construction administration and oversight
  • Start-up and commissioning

Document services

  • Complete detailed design, engineered and specified construction drawing packages
  • Concept design and study packages
  • Resource allocation studies

Design Wall

Design Wall is a collage of our work that demonstrates the potential and possibilities for water in design, and is intended to serve as a source for reference and inspiration. It will continue to grow and be populated with each new design and project completed.
